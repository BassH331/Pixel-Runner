The Story in 5 Chapters

The Grand Chronicle

A fast-paced journey from the fatal accident to the final battle on the steps of the gods.

Chapter 00 // The Accord

The Hole in Death's Rules

The gods of death divided all dying people between themselves like departments in an office. If you die in battle, one god takes you. If you get sick, another takes you. But nobody ever wrote a rule for someone who dies while someone else is trying to save them! It was a tiny blind spot in the universe. Voragis, a minor god of unpaid loans, quietly claimed that gap and built his entire empire on it.

“Voragis doesn't cause accidents. He just shows up when nobody else has the paperwork to claim you.”
Chapter 01 // The Accident

The Snap of the Oak & The Deal

You were walking home with your wife, Elise. Without warning—CRACK!—a giant ancient oak tree crashes down! You lunge and grab her wrist. You pull with all your might, but the ground crumbles and her fingers slip away like autumn leaves. Because it was a real rescue attempt, no normal god collects her. Out of the fog steps The Ledger—a mysterious man with a heavy iron book who offers you a fragment of demon power.

“Take this power. Climb the mountain. You get one chance to buy her back. But remember: power in, self out!”
Chapter 02 // The Dark Forest

Why Skeletons Are Just Empty Cans

The forest is crawling with skeletons and blood zombies. But here is the crazy secret: they aren't random monsters! They are people who signed the deal before you and failed. Voragis already drained their souls years ago. Slicing them up costs him nothing—they are like empty soda cans after the drink is finished! Then you meet the Dark Ronin, a samurai who liked the demon power so much he decided to keep it forever!

“Fail, and you become scenery. Win and choose wrong, and you become the boss's staff.”
Chapter 03 // The Sanctuary

The Silver Priestess & The Green Gargoyle

In a quiet grove glowing with blue crystals, you meet the Moonstone Keeper. She whispers that Candora, the Goddess of Love, is watching you. If you choose peace at the end, she will protect Elise's soul. But before you can leave the forest, you must face the Gatekeeper—a massive green winged gargoyle who blocks speedrunners and only lets you pass if you actually helped people along the way!

“You can't speedrun the woods. The giant gargoyle only respects those who carried the forest's weight.”
Chapter 04 // The Realm of Gods

The Scales of the Final Boss

High above the clouds on giant floating marble stairs stands The Wizard! He holds glowing golden scales that measure how much human heart you still have left. After an epic lightning duel, you shatter his staff. Now, you stand before the ultimate choice: surrender the power to be human again, or become the new immortal god of the forest!

“You reached the top before the demon ate your mind. Now make the choice that defines eternity.”

The Two Endings

Neither path is flagged as good or evil. Both cost something real; both gain something forever.

Debt Paid In Full

Peace Under Silver Skies

“You reach inside your chest and tear out the demon shard. You collapse onto the stone, fully human again.”

You give the power back. Your razor claws vanish, leaving ordinary, scarred hands. Elise cannot come back to live in the dirt—that was a false dream—but she is no longer trapped in the cold dark void! Candora cradles her soul in warm silver light. Elise smiles at you, knowing you stayed the man she loved, and crosses peacefully into paradise.

  • What You Gain: Elise finds eternal peace. Your soul is saved.
  • What It Costs: All demon powers are gone forever. You live a normal, mortal life with an empty home.

“You brought me home.”

Her mountain-water laugh returns to your memory. You are weak and fragile again, but your love was not in vain.

Permanent Promotion

The Sovereign of the Dark Forest

“You stop fighting the shadow. The dark armor hardens into your skin. You let the power take over completely.”

Why return to being weak and fragile? You accept the demon's permanent terms. The shard wraps around your spine, wiping away your mortal tears and erasing her face from your memory. Your eyes ignite with purple fire. You turn around and descend back into the forest as an immortal dark sovereign. When the next tragic accident happens, you will be the one holding the heavy ledger book!

  • What You Gain: Godlike immortality, shadow magic, and eternal strength!
  • What It Costs: You forget Elise completely. You become the next monster of the woods.

“The debt is settled.”

You mirror the Dark Ronin. When a new traveler cries over a fallen loved one, it is your shadow stepping through the trees to offer the deal.
